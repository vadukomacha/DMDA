 ===== WEEK 7: CLUSTERING — HIERARCHICAL CLUSTERER =====

 Step 1: Open Weka Explorer → Preprocess tab → Open file → select week7.txt
 Step 2: Click "Cluster" tab
 Step 3: Choose → HierarchicalClusterer → check "Store clusters for visualization" → click Start
 Step 4: In "Result list" (left panel), right-click on HierarchicalClusterer result → click "Visualize tree"

 Note: Make sure "Store clusters for visualization" is checked BEFORE clicking Start.
       Output shows clustered instances and merge distances.
       Visualize tree shows the dendrogram (hierarchical merge tree).
